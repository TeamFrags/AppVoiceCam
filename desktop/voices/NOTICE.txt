The voices named kokoro-*.wav are not recordings of anyone. They were spoken by the Kokoro
speech synthesiser (Kokoro-82M v1.0, https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), which is
published under the Apache License, Version 2.0, through kokoro-js 1.2.1 and its ONNX export
(https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX).

Each file is the same English text spoken with one of Kokoro's voices:

  kokoro-sky.wav      af_sky
  kokoro-kore.wav     af_kore
  kokoro-river.wav    af_river
  kokoro-emma.wav     bf_emma
  kokoro-bella.wav    af_bella
  kokoro-jessica.wav  af_jessica

Modification: the output of the synthesiser was stored as 16-bit PCM instead of 32-bit float.
desktop/scripts/make-voices.mjs makes them again.
