The voices named qwen-*.wav are not recordings of anyone. They were spoken by the Qwen3-TTS
speech synthesiser (https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS), which is published under the Apache
License, Version 2.0, through its qwen-tts Python package.

Each file is the same English text. Two were spoken with voices the synthesiser comes with
(https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-CustomVoice):

  qwen-vivian.wav  Vivian
  qwen-anna.wav    Ono_Anna

The five others were spoken with voices the synthesiser made up from a written description
(https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-VoiceDesign): no child and no adult was
recorded for them.

  qwen-deep.wav    a woman in her forties, deep voice
  qwen-warm.wav    a woman in her thirties, warm voice
  qwen-bright.wav  a woman in her twenties, bright voice
  qwen-boy.wav     an eleven-year-old boy
  qwen-girl.wav    a six-year-old girl

Modification: the output of the synthesiser was stored as 16-bit PCM instead of 32-bit float.
desktop/scripts/make-voices-qwen.py makes them again.
